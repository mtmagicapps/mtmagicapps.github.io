Privacy policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · App: VAG Scope · Developer: VAG Scope developer · Contact: through the Google Play store page

In short

VAG Scope does not collect, send or sell any data. The app has no internet permission, no account, no advertising and no analytics. Everything it reads or stores stays on your phone unless you decide to share it. The app itself never connects to the internet; the only web links in it open in your browser when you tap them (see "Links to online shops").

What the app reads

  • From your car, through your OBD-II adapter: measuring values, fault codes, freeze-frame data, control-unit identification, distance counters and the vehicle identification number (VIN).
  • From your phone: the Bluetooth name and address of the adapter you choose, and diagnostic procedures you pick yourself.

What is stored on your phone

Settings, the last adapter used, the groups and names you chose, dashboards, procedures and their run reports, recordings (CSV), fault backups made before clearing and the previous values saved before a coding, an adaptation or a service reset, and the mileage-check cache. This data is kept in the app's private storage, is excluded from Android cloud backups and device transfer, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Several screens also let you delete it.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you tap Share or Export. The file or text then goes to the app you choose (e-mail, messaging, cloud storage...), and that app's privacy policy applies. Exports and reports can contain your VIN and your car's data; check them before sharing. Settings › Shorten the VIN on screen hides most of it on screen.

Links to online shops

The "Recommended adapters" page (Connect screen, and Settings › Adapter) lists OBD-II adapters with links to online shops. A link opens in your web browser (or the app that handles it) only when you tap it. The app makes no connection itself and adds nothing of yours to the link: no VIN, no car data, no phone data. The shop's website is then covered by its own privacy policy and may use cookies or log your visit.

Some of these links are referral links. If you buy through one, the developer may receive a commission from the shop at no extra cost to you. What the shop's affiliate programme reports to the developer about such a purchase is governed by the shop's policy; the developer does not receive your identity from the app.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to find and talk to your OBD-II adapter.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only): Android requires it to scan for Bluetooth Low Energy adapters. The app never reads your location.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep the adapter connected while values are shown on Android Auto.

Purchase

The app is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's terms and privacy policy; the developer does not receive your payment details or your identity from the app.

Android Auto

Values are shown on your car's screen through Android Auto. The app sends nothing to Google; Android Auto itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

As the developer receives no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You keep full control of the data on your phone. For any question, contact the developer through the app's Google Play store page.

Changes

A new version of this policy will be published in the app and on its store page, with a new effective date.
