VAG Scope compares the odometer with the distance stored in the car’s control units and shows its evidence. It also checks the VIN, reads fault codes and live data, and changes coding, from your phone and a Bluetooth ELM327 adapter. No account, no network.

Check the odometer against the car’s own memory.

VAG Scope reads the distance stored in every control unit that keeps one, compares it with the dashboard, and shows its working: which units agree, which do not, and the margin it allowed.

The VIN check does the same for the car’s identity: it compares the VIN on the windscreen, the data sticker and the body with the VIN stored in every control unit, and names any unit that comes from another car. Both checks give a PDF report.

Handy before you buy a used car. The result is a guide, never a guarantee, and it will not tell you a car is “genuine”.

Questions before you buy

Which cars does VAG Scope work with?

Volkswagen, Audi, Seat and Škoda cars that use TP 2.0 (about 2004–2010) or UDS (about 2012 and later), and cars that mix both. It does not work on the newest platforms that use 29-bit diagnostic ids only, such as the ID. electric models and the Golf 8 generation.

Which adapter do I need?

A Bluetooth ELM327 adapter that plugs into the car’s OBD socket. On Android, Classic Bluetooth and BLE adapters both work. The app was built and tested with a vLinker MC+. The iPhone version, when it comes, will need a BLE adapter.

Can it tell me if the mileage has been tampered with?

It compares the odometer with the distance stored in every control unit that keeps one, and shows which units agree, which do not, and the margin it allowed. A disagreement is a reason to look closer. An agreement is not proof: someone can change several units. Treat the result as one clue among others, next to the service history and an inspection.

Does it need internet or an account?

No. VAG Scope has no internet permission, no account, no advertising and no analytics. What your car says stays on your phone.

How much does it cost?

One payment on Google Play, €9.99 in the euro zone. No subscription, no in-app purchase, every feature included.

Is there an iPhone version?

It is coming later. Sign up at the top of this page to be told when it is out.

Can it damage my car?

Reading fault codes, live data and the mileage changes nothing. Every change, from clearing a fault to coding, asks for your confirmation first, is backed up and read back afterwards, and airbag and immobiliser units are never written. Never use the app while driving.
